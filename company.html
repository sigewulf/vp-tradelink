A manufacturer knows its own product deeply. A conventional trader may offer many products, but does not always work across the full range of specifications and sources available for the same product. We work with multiple origins and suppliers for the same products, giving us a broader understanding of available specifications, qualities, and sourcing options. Payment & Quality

How payment and quality assurance work.

Payment terms

We work on Letter of Credit (LC), Telegraphic Transfer (TT), or Cash Against Documents (CAD), and agree the specific structure with each buyer before confirming an order — for example, an advance against TT, or documents released through a bank under CAD. If you have a payment structure your company requires, tell us early and we'll confirm whether it fits.

Quality & inspection

Every shipment ships against an agreed specification and a Certificate of Analysis. Third-party inspection (SGS, Intertek or an agency of your choice) can be arranged at the buyer's cost before loading, so you have an independent result in hand — not just ours — before the container leaves port. If a shipment doesn't match the agreed COA, that discrepancy is handled directly with you against the inspection report; raise it as soon as it's identified so it can be resolved while the shipment is still traceable.
