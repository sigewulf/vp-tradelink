Values shown are commercial ranges. Na₂O content distinguishes this from Potash Feldspar and is the number glass and sanitaryware manufacturers check first, since sodium fluxes at a lower temperature than potassium and shifts the melting/firing curve. If you're unsure which of the two feldspars your formulation calls for, tell us the target firing range and we can recommend accordingly.
